# TrackAura - Your Personal Wellness Companion

**TrackAura** is a web-based fitness application designed to help users reach their health goals. Whether you want to lose, maintain, or gain weight, TrackAura calculates your specific needs and tracks your progress throughout the day.

---

## Key Features
* **Dynamic Dashboard:** Real-time visualization of calories consumed vs. calories burned.
* **Activity Logging:** Log workouts (Walking, Running, Cycling) with automatic step estimation.
* **Smart Goals:** Automatic BMI calculation and personalized calorie targets.
* **Meal Tracking:** Categorized meal logging with instant calorie updates.
* **Hydration Tracker:** Easy-to-use water intake counter.

---

## Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** React.js, Context API, Axios
* **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
* **Database:** MongoDB
* **Styling:** Custom CSS3
